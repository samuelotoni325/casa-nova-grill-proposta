November 1992 – today

34 years carving on Ferry Street.

Casa Nova Grill opened in November 1992, started by pioneer restaurateur José Moreira. In the restaurant’s own words, it has become synonymous with Brazilian culture in the Ironbound section of Newark.

Same corner, same fire: a 250-seat dining room, a full bar, a private party room and a parking lot across the street.
